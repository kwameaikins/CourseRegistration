# Knowsia Growth Partner Programme

**Tagline:** *Learn. Refer. Earn. Grow.*

My strongest recommendation is a **hybrid affiliate and strategic partnership model**. Individual promoters should earn performance-based commissions, while institutions, tutors, professional associations and corporate partners receive arrangements suited to the value they provide.

## 1. Main Objectives

The programme should help Knowsia:

- Acquire students without paying large advertising costs upfront.
- Build relationships with tutors, universities, professional bodies, accounting firms and training centres.
- Reward existing students and professionals for recommendations.
- Generate recurring subscription revenue.
- Track every referral, payment, commission and payout transparently.
- Turn high-performing affiliates into long-term regional partners.

## 2. The Four Partner Categories

| Partner category | Best candidates | Primary reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Knowsia Ambassadors** | Students, alumni, content creators, accountants and professionals | Percentage commission |
| **Tutor Partners** | Approved Knowsia tutors and facilitators | Referral commission plus teaching compensation |
| **Institutional Partners** | Tuition centres, universities, churches, associations and accounting firms | Student discount plus fixed referral payment |
| **Strategic Partners** | Professional bodies, employers, NGOs and large communities | Negotiated revenue share or bulk contract |

### A. Knowsia Ambassadors

These are individuals who promote programmes through WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok, email, events and personal networks.

#### Recommended Commission Structure

| Tier | Monthly paid enrolments | Course commission | Subscription commission |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Starter** | 1–9 | 12% | 10% of first payment |
| **Growth** | 10–29 | 15% | 10% first payment + 5% renewal for 3 months |
| **Elite** | 30+ | 18% | 15% first payment + 5% renewal for 6 months |

Each ambassador should receive:

- A unique referral link.
- A personal discount code.
- A downloadable QR code.
- A partner dashboard.
- Approved promotional materials.
- Monthly performance reports.
- Access to partner training.
- Recognition and achievement badges.

#### Customer Incentive

Give learners referred by an ambassador a **5% discount**.

The affiliate commission must be calculated on the amount actually collected after discounts, refunds and chargebacks—not on the advertised course fee.

#### Example

For a GH¢800 programme:

- Listed fee: GH¢800
- Customer discount at 5%: GH¢40
- Amount collected: GH¢760
- Growth Partner commission at 15%: GH¢114
- Knowsia balance before payment fees and course delivery costs: **GH¢646**

This means the combined acquisition cost is GH¢154, or approximately 19.25% of the original course price.

### B. Tutor Partners

Every approved tutor should automatically receive affiliate capability through the tutor account.

#### Recommended Tutor-Referral Arrangement

- **10% of the referred learner’s first subscription payment.**
- **5% of subscription renewals for six months.**
- A separate referral reward for learners brought into the tutor’s live programme.
- Referral earnings displayed separately from teaching compensation.
- Affiliate commission calculated after direct discounts, refunds and payment reversals.

Tutor compensation and referral commission should not be combined into one unclear percentage. The dashboard should separately show:

1. Minimum guarantee.
2. Live teaching compensation.
3. Recorded-course revenue share.
4. Referral commission.
5. Bonuses.
6. Approved payout balance.

For recorded courses, the tutor may receive a higher revenue share for sales they personally generate, but the total payout should remain within the agreed recorded-course revenue ceiling.

### C. Institutional Partners

This category should cover tuition centres, professional associations, universities, churches, employers and accounting firms.

#### Recommended Institutional Structure

| Level | Annual students | Partner payment | Student discount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Partner** | 1–49 | GH¢30 per paid learner | 10% |
| **Gold Partner** | 50–199 | GH¢40 per paid learner | 15% |
| **Platinum Partner** | 200+ | GH¢50 per paid learner | 20% |

Institutional partners should receive:

- A branded registration page.
- A unique institutional discount code.
- Monthly enrolment reports.
- Co-branded flyers and banners.
- Group onboarding sessions.
- A dedicated partnership contact.
- Certificates showing both Knowsia and the partner’s name, where appropriate.
- Priority access to new programmes.
- The opportunity to request private cohorts.

Payments should be based only on learners who have paid and passed the refund or cancellation period.

### D. Strategic Partners

Strategic partnerships are broader than ordinary affiliate relationships. They could include:

- ICAG tuition providers.
- Accounting and audit firms.
- Universities and business schools.
- Professional associations.
- Corporate HR and learning departments.
- Banks and financial institutions.
- NGOs and youth-development organisations.

The arrangement may include:

- Co-branded programmes.
- Corporate training contracts.
- Sponsored scholarships.
- Joint events and webinars.
- Bulk learner subscriptions.
- Revenue sharing.
- Lead exchange arrangements.
- Regional representation.

Strategic partners should not be placed under the normal affiliate commission table. Each partnership should have a signed agreement specifying responsibilities, ownership of leads, branding rights, revenue distribution and programme delivery.

## 3. Attribution Rules

Referral attribution is where many affiliate programmes develop disputes. Knowsia should adopt clear rules from the beginning.

### Referral Window

Use a **30-day attribution period** from the affiliate’s most recent valid referral click.

### Attribution Priority

1. Unique partner discount code entered at checkout.
2. Tracked affiliate link.
3. QR code connected to the partner account.
4. Manual claim submitted within 48 hours, supported by evidence.

### Existing Leads

No commission should be paid where:

- The learner was already in an active Knowsia sales process before the referral.
- The learner previously purchased the same programme.
- An employee manually assigns an existing customer to an affiliate.
- The affiliate refers themselves using another account.
- The transaction is refunded, reversed or fraudulent.

### Commission Status

Every commission should move through:

**Tracked → Pending → Approved → Payable → Paid**

A pending period of **14 days** is advisable before a course-sale commission becomes payable.

## 4. Payout System

Set a minimum affiliate payout balance of **GH¢100**.

Recommended payout cycle:

- Commission closes on the final day of each month.
- Finance reviews commissions during the first five working days.
- Approved payouts are processed by the 10th working day.
- Partners receive a downloadable statement.
- Reversed or refunded transactions appear clearly in the statement.

Partners should be able to select:

- Mobile money.
- Bank account.

Knowsia can use a payment provider such as Paystack for payment collection and partner payouts while maintaining the commission ledger inside the Knowsia platform.

## 5. Partner Recruitment Strategy

Do not accept everyone automatically. A weak affiliate can damage the Knowsia brand.

### Ideal First Recruitment Group

Start with approximately:

- 20 existing students and alumni.
- 10 tutors and facilitators.
- 10 accounting or finance content creators.
- 5 tuition centres.
- 5 professional or student associations.
- 5 corporate or institutional partners.

This creates a controlled pilot of approximately **55 partners**.

### Application Requirements

Applicants should provide:

- Full name and contact information.
- Social-media or community links.
- Professional background.
- Intended promotional methods.
- Estimated audience size.
- Bank or mobile-money payout details.
- Agreement to the partner code of conduct.

Partners should be approved manually during the pilot.

## 6. Partner Marketing System

Each month, Knowsia should give partners a campaign package containing:

- Campaign objective.
- Course description.
- Target audience.
- Approved WhatsApp messages.
- LinkedIn and Facebook posts.
- Short video scripts.
- Flyers in different sizes.
- Frequently asked questions.
- Registration deadlines.
- Discount code.
- Unique tracking link.
- Suggested follow-up messages.

Use a campaign sequence rather than sending only one flyer.

### Recommended Sequence

**Awareness:** Explain the professional problem the programme solves.

**Education:** Share a practical lesson, case study, checklist or short video.

**Evidence:** Publish testimonials, student results or facilitator credentials.

**Offer:** Present the programme, fee, discount and registration deadline.

**Urgency:** Communicate remaining time or available places honestly.

**Follow-up:** Contact interested people who have not completed payment.

Partners should never be permitted to invent course benefits, fake deadlines, guaranteed employment claims or unapproved discounts.

## 7. Partner Dashboard Requirements

The Knowsia affiliate module should show:

- Unique links, codes and QR codes.
- Clicks and landing-page visits.
- Leads generated.
- Completed registrations.
- Paid enrolments.
- Conversion rate.
- Pending commissions.
- Approved commissions.
- Reversed commissions.
- Available payout balance.
- Payment history.
- Performance by campaign and course.
- UTM tracking.
- Partner tier and progress.
- Marketing-resource library.
- Payout request function.
- Dispute or support function.

Administrators should be able to see:

- Top-performing partners.
- Suspicious transactions.
- Duplicate accounts.
- Refund rates.
- Conversion by partner.
- Revenue generated.
- Commission cost.
- Outstanding payouts.
- Partner profitability.
- Campaign and course performance.

## 8. Anti-Fraud Controls

The programme terms should prohibit:

- Self-referrals.
- Fake student accounts.
- Cookie stuffing.
- Trademark bidding without permission.
- Misleading advertisements.
- Spam messages.
- Buying contact lists.
- Claiming another partner’s referrals.
- Offering unauthorised cashback.
- Creating fake scarcity.
- Posting confidential course materials.
- Registering customers without consent.

Affiliates must not scrape WhatsApp numbers or add people to marketing groups without permission.

Knowsia should reserve the right to:

- Hold suspicious commissions.
- Request supporting evidence.
- Reverse fraudulent earnings.
- Suspend partner accounts.
- Permanently terminate serious offenders.

## 9. Performance Bonuses

Use bonuses to reward consistent performance rather than permanently offering excessive commissions.

Examples:

- **First Five Bonus:** GH¢100 after the first five verified enrolments.
- **Monthly Champion:** GH¢300 for the highest-performing qualifying partner.
- **Fast Start Bonus:** Additional 3% for approved partners who achieve ten enrolments in their first 30 days.
- **Institutional Milestone:** One complimentary course seat after 25 paid learners.
- **Retention Bonus:** Additional payment where referred subscription students remain active for six months.

Avoid paying commissions for clicks, WhatsApp enquiries or unqualified leads during the first phase. Pay mainly for completed and verified transactions.

## 10. Ninety-Day Launch Plan

### Days 1–14: Build the Foundation

- Finalise commission percentages.
- Draft affiliate terms and partner agreements.
- Create registration and approval processes.
- Configure referral links, codes and QR codes.
- Establish CRM lead-source rules.
- Build the commission ledger.
- Prepare marketing materials.
- Train finance and marketing staff.

### Days 15–45: Run the Pilot

- Recruit the first 55 partners.
- Conduct a live partner orientation.
- Give every partner a starter campaign kit.
- Hold weekly partner-support sessions.
- Review attribution disputes manually.
- Test mobile-money and bank payouts.
- Identify the ten most promising partners.

### Days 46–90: Scale

- Introduce partner tiers.
- Launch the partner leaderboard.
- Publish partner success stories.
- Recruit more institutional partners.
- Introduce co-branded landing pages.
- Automate commission approval and statements.
- Assign a dedicated Partner Success Manager.
- Expand the programme after the pilot is profitable.

## 11. Key Performance Indicators

The programme should be judged by:

| Area | KPI |
|---|---|
| Recruitment | Number of approved partners |
| Activation | Percentage generating at least one lead |
| Productivity | Paid learners per active partner |
| Conversion | Paid learners divided by referred leads |
| Revenue | Net revenue generated by partners |
| Cost | Discounts plus commissions as a percentage of revenue |
| Quality | Refund and chargeback rate |
| Retention | Subscription renewal rate |
| Operations | Percentage of payouts completed on time |
| Concentration | Percentage of revenue coming from the top five partners |
| Compliance | Number of policy violations or disputes |

A partner should be considered active only when they generate meaningful traffic, a qualified lead or a paid enrolment—not merely because they registered.

## Final Recommendation

Launch Knowsia’s programme as a **controlled hybrid partnership system**, not as an open “refer anybody and earn” scheme.

Begin with:

- 20 student and professional ambassadors.
- 10 tutors.
- 10 creators.
- 10 institutional or strategic partners.
- A 30-day attribution window.
- A 14-day commission validation period.
- Monthly payouts.
- A maximum normal customer-acquisition cost of approximately **20–25% of collected revenue**.

The strongest long-term advantage will not come from offering the highest commission. It will come from giving partners reliable tracking, good products, quality campaign materials, transparent payments and a clear pathway from ordinary ambassador to strategic Knowsia partner.
